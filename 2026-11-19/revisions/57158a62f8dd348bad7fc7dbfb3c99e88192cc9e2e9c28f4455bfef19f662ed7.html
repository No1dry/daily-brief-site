LogT: Logically Think with Images for Visual Search

👍 2

HuggingFace Trending Papers · 11/14 08:00

Thinking with images empowers vision-language models (VLMs) to use an external Zoom-in tool for fine-grained visual search. Recent works employ supervised fine-tuning (SFT) and reinforcement learning (RL) with tool-specific designs to enable such capabilities. However, achieving logical reasoning alongside accurate and efficient tool use remains a significant challenge. To address this, we shift the focus away from increasing RL complexity and instead identify SFT data quality as a key bottleneck. We then propose LogT (short for Logically Think with Images), a fully automated SFT data engine that explicitly enforces logic-chain-guided reasoning. LogT generates an SFT dataset featuring challenging, visually grounded questions and structured tool-use traces with human-like logic. We validate our approach by fine-tuning two base models, Qwen2.5-VL-7B and InternVL3.5-8B, on the dataset and evaluating them across seven benchmark test sets. We show that LogT-SFT based on Qwen2.5-VL-7B outperforms the state-of-the-art trained with both SFT and RL, despite using 3.1times less data and >120times fewer GPU hours. Furthermore, applying a simple RL recipe without tool-specific design on LogT-SFT yields a model that surpasses prior methods with complex RL setups by a substantial 9.59-point margin. In addition to strong performance gains, our model exhibits higher tool-use accuracy and efficiency, as well as more structured and logically consistent reasoning traces, directly validating the effectiveness of our approach.

中文介绍 针对 VLM 借助 Zoom-in 工具做细粒度视觉搜索时难以兼顾逻辑推理与准确高效工具调用的问题，将重点从增加 RL 复杂度转向 SFT 数据质量，提出全自动数据引擎 LogT，显式强制逻辑链引导的推理，生成含视觉 grounded 难题与类人逻辑工具调用轨迹的数据集。在 Qwen2.5-VL-7B 与 InternVL3.5-8B 上微调并在七个基准测试：LogT-SFT 用少 3.1 倍数据、少 120 倍以上 GPU 小时即超过 SFT+RL 的 SOTA；再加简单 RL 配方后领先复杂 RL 方案 9.59 个百分点。

Game2Player: Scaling Gameplay Experience for Visual Agents through Environment-Player Co-Development

👍 18

HuggingFace Trending Papers · 11/18 08:00

Games are a rich testbed for visual agents, demanding perception, long-horizon planning, and precise control. However, collecting human gameplay is costly, and learning from gameplay videos requires inferring missing actions. Existing datasets also cover a limited range of games, often without explicit tasks or verifiable outcomes. To address these gaps, we introduce Game2Player, a framework that co-develops game environments and players to collect diverse and verifiable gameplay experience at scale. In the Game2Player loop, a Builder agent adapts existing web games, exposes their internal state, and defines tasks with state-based progress measures and success checks. A Coding Player and a VLM Player then attempt the tasks and report problems to the Builder for repair, so that play serves as both environment validation and training data collection. With Game2Player, we build 568 verified web games with 3,436 tasks, collect 15.7K trajectories (307 hours of gameplay), and hold out 50 games as the benchmark PlayerBench. To evaluate the collected experience as training data, we train VLM agents with progress-weighted SFT, assigning greater weight to steps that advance task progress.
