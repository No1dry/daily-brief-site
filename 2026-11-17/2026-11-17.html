K2-Horizon: A Frontier Fleet of Open Source Language Models

👍 10

HuggingFace Trending Papers · 11/16 08:00

We present K2-Horizon, a fleet of six fully open language models, from a 0.9B dense model to a 375B-A23B MoE, built for general-purpose reasoning and agentic work across math and science, software engineering, deep research, office automation, and beyond. The models lead or are competitive with strong open models of their size on agentic and coding benchmarks. On the modeling side, to strengthen reasoning, we introduce Mixture-of-Value Attention (MoVA), applying MoE to attention value projections, and softplus attention gating, a more flexible, theoretically motivated gate. Our 36B-A4B model uses both and nearly matches a 32B dense model trained on the same data, with one-seventh the active parameters. We also introduce Uno, lossless diffusion adapters that speed up decoding by up to 2.2x without a separate draft model. Our release follows the LLM360 definition of open source for transparency and reproducibility: the full development history, including intermediate checkpoints from every training stage, pre-training data, training logs, and all training, evaluation, and infrastructure code, notably our production-scale xLLM framework for dense and MoE training and the RL360 reinforcement-learning stack. Beyond the data itself, we release and document in detail every pipeline that built it, the part of model development that open releases most rarely disclose. These pipelines produced roughly 10T synthetic pre-training tokens, showing that synthetic data can be a major ingredient of pre-training at scale. Four of the models share one 22T-token pre-training corpus and recipe, revealing consistent scaling behavior and enabling controlled studies of scale and sparsity. All artifacts are available at https://ifm.ai/k2/

中文介绍 K2-Horizon 发布 6 个全开放语言模型，覆盖 0.9B 稠密到 375B-A23B MoE，面向数学、科学、软件工程与智能体任务。提出 Mixture-of-Value Attention（MoVA）与 softplus 注意力门控，36B-A4B 模型以约七分之一激活参数接近同等数据训练的 32B 稠密模型；Uno 无损扩散适配器提速解码至多 2.2 倍。按 LLM360 标准公开全训练历史、约 10T 合成预训练 token 及全部数据流水线。

MIRIX-2.0: Multi-Agent Memory System

👍 49

HuggingFace Trending Papers · 11/09 08:00

Long-term memory lets LLM agents personalize, stay consistent, and reuse what worked before. Parametric memories need training, consolidation can lose details for good, and keeping every record leaves a growing store to search. We present MIRIX-2.0, a multi-agent memory system designed to be practical, accurate, and efficient at once. It is the most accurate of the compared systems on four of five QA benchmarks. It reaches 91.4% on LoCoMo and 72.6% on LongMemEval-S, where it leads the strongest baseline by 14.3 points, and with AutoDream disabled it gains 31.0 and 22.0 points over HyperMem on single-hop and multi-hop document QA. As procedural memory, it yields the highest mean success among the compared memory methods on ALFWorld and WebShop and reaches 49.2% on MetaClaw-Bench, against 44.0% for MetaClaw's skill library. Six typed memory agents keep complete records as the system of record, and a provenance hypergraph indexes episodic and semantic records with n-ary frames that cite their rows. Retrieval runs under explicit budgets, so the evidence a question inspects does not grow with the history. AutoDream merges duplicate anchors outside the write path without rewriting any record, and it distills task sessions into skills. With AutoDream disabled, MIRIX-2.0 answers faster than HyperMem on all five timed benchmarks. It needs no fine-tuning, runs with closed and open models, and its code is public.

WebArena-Pro: A Heterogeneous, Multimodal, Reproducible Benchmark for Web Agents

👍 12

HuggingFace Trending Papers · 11/13 08:00

Web agents powered by large language and vision-language models are increasingly applied to realistic browser work that spans heterogeneous applications, multimodal content, and stateful workflows. However, existing reproducible web-agent benchmarks cover only a small number of web applications drawn from a few software categories, and restrict modality to text and vision. Live benchmarks broaden site coverage but sacrifice reproducibility, since pages and data drift between runs. Moreover, existing benchmarks do not meaningfully evaluate whether agents can understand and use audio and video content embedded within web tasks. To address these gaps, we introduce WebArena-Pro, a benchmark comprising 300 tasks across 20 self-hosted web applications in six domain categories, spanning distinct interface conventions, workflows, and data models. Across the evaluated agents, the best performance is achieved by both Codex with GPT 6.1 Sol and Claude Code with Claude Opus 5.5 at 62.3%, followed by OpenCode with Kimi 3.1 Pro at 60.7%. The strongest GenericAgent configurations, Gemini 3.1 Pro omni and Gemma4 31B, both achieve 30.3%. Among reproducible, human-curated web agent benchmarks, WebArena-Pro provides the broadest application coverage and the most comprehensive multimodal support to date. The benchmark treats audio and video as core observations alongside text and vision, with dedicated actions for extracting information from each.
